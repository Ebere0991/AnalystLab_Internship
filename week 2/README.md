# AnalystLab Africa – Data Analytics Internship

## Week 2 – Global Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project focused on analyzing a global sales dataset to understand overall business performance, sales trends, profitability, product performance, customer segments, shipping costs, and regional performance.

The analysis involved preparing and exploring the dataset, performing statistical and pivot table analysis, identifying outliers, and transforming the findings into interactive Power BI dashboards.

The overall purpose was to generate meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making and help management understand the factors influencing sales and profitability.

## 2. Objectives

The key objectives of this project were to:

- Clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Explore the dataset and identify important trends and patterns.
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis.
- Identify potential outliers and unusual observations.
- Analyze sales and profitability across products, categories, segments, regions, and other business dimensions.
- Create pivot tables to summarize key business metrics.
- Develop interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Communicate key findings and business implications through clear visualizations.

## 3. Dataset

The dataset contains global sales and business performance information across different products, customer segments, categories, regions, and shipping methods.

The dataset was inspected and prepared before analysis to improve data quality and ensure that the information used for visualization and reporting was reliable.

## 5. Power BI Dashboard

The cleaned and analyzed dataset was used to create interactive Power BI dashboards for business performance monitoring.

Two dashboard views were developed:

### A. Executive Performance Summary

The executive dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance through key performance indicators and visualizations.

It includes:

- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Average Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Sales
- Profit by Product Category
- Sales Performance by Region
- Sales Distribution by Category
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Profit by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Category Performance Summary

### B. Product, Profitability & Cost Analysis

The second dashboard provides a more detailed view of product and profitability performance.

It includes:

- Shipping Cost by Ship Mode
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Top 10 Products by Sales

The dashboards were designed to allow users to explore performance across different categories and customer segments and identify areas requiring further attention.

## 6. Key Findings

The analysis revealed several important business findings:

1. **Technology generated the highest profit among the three major product categories**, outperforming Office Supplies and Furniture.

2. **The Consumer segment recorded the highest sales**, followed by Corporate and Home Office customers.

3. **Regional performance varied considerably**, with the Central region recording the highest sales and profit among the regions shown in the dashboard.

4. **Shipping costs differed substantially by shipping mode**, with Standard Class accounting for the largest shipping cost.

5. **Product and sub-category performance varied significantly**, with a small number of products generating considerably higher sales and profits than others.

## 7. Business Implications

The findings indicate that business performance is concentrated around specific categories, products, customer segments, and regions. Management can use these insights to prioritize high-performing products and markets while reviewing underperforming areas. Shipping costs and discount levels should also be monitored carefully because they can affect overall profitability. A data-driven approach to product strategy, regional expansion, and cost management can help improve business performance.
