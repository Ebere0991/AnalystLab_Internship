# AnalystLab Africa Internship

Welcome to my **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship** repository.

This repository contains the projects, analysis, reports, visualizations, and other practical work completed throughout my **8-week Data Analytics Internship**. Each week's folder contains the work completed during that stage of the internship, along with supporting documentation and screenshots where applicable.

The projects demonstrate my application of data analytics concepts to real-world business problems, from data preparation and exploration to visualization, insights, and business recommendations.

## 📊 Week 1 – Telco Customer Churn Analysis

Week 1 focused on analyzing a **Telecommunication Customer Churn dataset** to understand customer behavior, identify factors associated with churn, uncover trends and patterns, and develop data-driven business recommendations.

The project involved data cleaning, outlier analysis, descriptive statistics, Pivot Table analysis, visualization, dashboard development, business insights, risks, opportunities, and recommendations.

**Tools used:** Microsoft Excel, Power BI, WPS Office
