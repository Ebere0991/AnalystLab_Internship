## Week 1 - **Business Analysis Case Study**

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics training program.

The project focuses on analyzing a telecommunications customer churn dataset to understand customer behavior, identify factors associated with customer churn, uncover trends and patterns, and provide actionable business recommendations that can support customer retention and business growth.

The project followed an end-to-end data analytics workflow, beginning with understanding the business problem and inspecting the dataset, followed by data cleaning, exploratory analysis, descriptive statistics, outlier analysis, data summarization, visualization, dashboard development, insight generation, and business recommendations.

Different tools were used at different stages of the project:

- **WPS Office** was used to prepare the Business Understanding Report.
- **Microsoft Excel** was used for data inspection, cleaning, outlier analysis, descriptive statistics, and Pivot Table analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI** was used to create the final interactive dashboard and visualize the findings.

The overall objective was to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that can help management understand churn and develop strategies to improve customer retention.

# Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecommunications industry.

When customers discontinue their services, businesses lose recurring revenue and may also incur additional costs to acquire new customers. Understanding why customers leave and identifying customer groups with higher churn rates can help organizations develop targeted retention strategies.

This project therefore investigates the characteristics and behavior of telco customers and explores the factors that may be associated with customer churn.

# Dataset

The dataset used for this project is a **Telco Customer Churn Dataset**.

The dataset contains information about telecom customers, their demographic characteristics, services, account information, payment methods, tenure, and churn status.

The dataset was analyzed to understand customer behavior and determine which customer segments and service characteristics are associated with higher levels of churn.

# Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer the following key business questions:

1. What does the customer base look like?

2. Which customer segments have the highest churn?

3. Does contract type influence customer retention?

4. Does customer tenure affect loyalty?

5. Which services are associated with higher customer churn?

6. Which payment method has a higher churn rate?

7. What actions should management take to reduce customer churn?

These questions guided the data exploration, analysis, visualization, and recommendation stages of the project.

# 1. Data Loading and Inspection

The dataset was initially inspected to understand its structure and identify potential data quality issues.

The inspection covered:

- Number of rows and columns
- Column names
- Data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Unique values
- Summary statistics
- Potential inconsistencies
- Unusual observations

This stage helped establish the overall quality and structure of the dataset before analysis.

# 2. Data Cleaning

The raw dataset was cleaned and prepared for analysis using Power Query.

Data cleaning activities included:

- Checking for missing values
- Identifying duplicate records
- Reviewing inconsistent entries
- Checking data types
- Standardizing data where necessary
- Reviewing unusual values
- Preparing the dataset for analysis

The objective of the cleaning process was to ensure that the dataset was consistent, reliable, and suitable for further analysis.

# 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was performed to understand the characteristics of the customer base and identify patterns related to churn.

The analysis examined customer characteristics and business variables including:

- Customer segments
- Churn status
- Contract type
- Tenure
- Services used
- Payment methods
- Other relevant customer attributes

Different analytical techniques and visualizations were used to identify relationships and patterns within the data.

# 4. Outlier Analysis

Outlier analysis was performed using Microsoft Excel to identify unusually high or low observations within relevant numerical variables.

The purpose of this analysis was to:

- Identify unusual observations.
- Detect potential data quality issues.
- Understand extreme customer values.
- Determine whether outliers could influence the analysis.
- Improve confidence in the analytical results.

The identified outliers were reviewed to determine whether they represented genuine customer behavior or potential data issues.
