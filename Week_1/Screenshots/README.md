# Week 1 Project Screenshots

## Overview

This folder contains screenshots demonstrating the work completed for the **AnalystLab Africa Week 1 Data Analytics Internship Project**.

The screenshots provide visual evidence of the different stages of the analysis, including data preparation, statistical analysis, exploratory analysis, visualization, reporting, and business recommendations.

## Contents

The screenshots in this folder cover the following areas:

- Cleaned Data
- Outlier Analysis
- Descriptive Statistics
- Pivot Table Analysis
- Power BI Dashboard
- Dataset Inspection Report
- Business Analytics Report

## 1. Cleaned Data

The cleaned data screenshot shows the dataset after the data preparation and cleaning process.

The cleaning process involved reviewing the dataset and addressing data quality issues such as:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Incorrect or inconsistent data
- Data formatting issues
- Data types and structure

The purpose of this stage was to ensure that the dataset was suitable for further analysis.

**Screenshot:**  
`01_Cleaned_Data.png`

## 2. Outlier Analysis

The outlier analysis screenshot demonstrates the identification and examination of unusual values within the dataset.

This step helped to:

- Identify unusual customer records
- Understand the distribution of numerical variables
- Determine whether extreme values required further investigation
- Improve the reliability of subsequent analysis

**Screenshot:**  
`02_Outliers.png`

## 3. Descriptive Statistics

The descriptive statistics screenshot presents the statistical summary of relevant numerical variables in the dataset.

The analysis included measures such as:

- Mean
- Median
- Minimum
- Maximum
- Standard deviation
- Count

These statistics helped provide an initial understanding of the dataset and the characteristics of the customer base.

## 4. Pivot Table Analysis

The pivot table screenshot demonstrates the use of Excel Pivot Tables to summarize and analyze the telecom customer data.

Pivot tables were used to examine customer churn across different categories and customer segments.

The analysis helped identify patterns and relationships involving factors such as:

- Customer demographics
- Contract type
- Tenure
- Services
- Payment methods
- Churn status

The results from the pivot table analysis contributed to the identification of key business insights.

**Screenshot:**  
`04_Pivot_Table.png`

## 5. Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard screenshot presents the visual analysis of the telecom customer churn dataset.

The dashboard was created to transform the analyzed data into interactive and easy-to-understand visualizations.

The dashboard was designed to help users understand:

- Overall customer churn
- Customer segments
- Churn patterns
- Customer characteristics
- Factors associated with customer retention and churn

The dashboard provides a visual summary of the findings and supports data-driven decision-making.

## 8. Business Analytics Report

The Business Analytics Report presents the major findings and conclusions from the customer churn analysis.

The report includes:

### Executive Summary

A summary of the overall analysis, major findings, and their business significance.

### Business Understanding

A description of the customer churn problem and the objectives of the analysis.

### Data Overview

An overview of the dataset and the variables used in the analysis.

### Key Insights and Observations

The major patterns and findings identified from the data analysis.

These findings were developed from the cleaned dataset, descriptive statistics, pivot table analysis, and visualizations.

### Business Risks

The analysis identified key risks associated with customer churn and customer retention.

### Business Opportunities

The findings were also used to identify opportunities that could help the organization improve customer retention and business performance.

### Business Recommendations

Actionable recommendations were developed based on the findings of the analysis.

The recommendations are intended to help management address customer churn, improve customer retention, and support better business decision-making.

### Conclusion

The report concludes the analysis by summarizing the major findings and their implications for the business.
