# HealthConnect Week 5 – Dashboard Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots of the Power BI analytical dashboard developed for the AnalystLab Africa HealthConnect Week 5 Data Analytics project.

The screenshots provide visual evidence of the completed dashboard and the key analyses conducted during the project.

## Dashboard Pages

### 01 – Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of appointment performance, including key KPIs, appointment outcomes, no-show patterns and major appointment characteristics.

### 02 – No-Show Drivers
Focuses on factors associated with missed appointments, including previous no-shows, appointment type, appointment time, age group and distance to the clinic.

### 03 – Patient Support and Appointment Operations
Examines appointment operations and patient-support factors, including reminder channels, waiting time, cancellations and appointment outcomes.

## Purpose

The screenshots serve as supporting evidence for the Power BI dashboard submitted as part of the Week 5 HealthConnect Data Analytics project.
