# Week 3 – Power BI Project Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots showcasing the key outputs and interactive pages developed for the Week 3 Power BI Customer Churn Analysis project completed during the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship.

## Dashboard Pages

The screenshots provide visual previews of the following Power BI report pages:

- **Executive Dashboard** – High-level overview of customer churn and business performance.
- **Customer Insights** – Analysis of customer characteristics, segments, and churn patterns.
- **Service Analysis** – Analysis of service usage and its relationship with customer churn.
- **Revenue Analysis** – Analysis of revenue performance and the financial impact of customer churn.
- **Customer Details** – Detailed customer-level view for deeper investigation.

## Purpose

These screenshots provide visual documentation of the Power BI dashboard and demonstrate the analytical and reporting work completed during the project.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
