# AnalystLab Africa – Data Analytics Internship

## Week 3 – Power BI Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

Week 3 focused on transforming the Telco Customer Churn dataset into an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution.

The project involved developing a multi-page Power BI dashboard to analyze customer churn, customer characteristics, service usage, revenue performance, and factors associated with customer retention and attrition.

The analysis was designed to answer key business questions around:

- Who is churning?
- Why are customers leaving?
- Which customer segments are at higher risk of churn?
- Which services are associated with customer churn?
- How does churn affect revenue?
- What actions can management take to improve customer retention?

The project also involved the development of custom DAX measures, interactive report features, and supporting business documentation.

## 2. Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze customer churn patterns and identify high-risk customer segments.
- Understand the characteristics of customers who churn.
- Identify services and customer attributes associated with churn.
- Analyze revenue performance and revenue at risk.
- Develop meaningful KPIs and business metrics using DAX.
- Create an interactive, professional, multi-page Power BI dashboard.
- Apply Power BI features such as slicers, drill-through, tooltips, bookmarks, and page navigation.
- Communicate findings and actionable recommendations through an Executive Insights Report.

## 3. Dataset

The project uses the Telco Customer Churn dataset.

The dataset contains information about telecommunications customers, their demographic characteristics, account information, subscribed services, billing details, and churn status.

### Key areas analyzed include:

- Customer demographics
- Customer tenure
- Contract type
- Payment method
- Internet service
- Phone service
- Additional subscribed services
- Monthly charges
- Total charges
- Churn status
- Customer-related attributes

The dataset was prepared and modeled in Power BI before the analysis and visualization stages.

## 4. Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI solution consists of five main pages, each designed to provide a different perspective of customer and business performance.

### 4.1 Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides a high-level overview of the organization's customer and churn performance.

It presents key KPIs and visualizations that allow management to quickly understand the overall state of the customer base, churn levels, and revenue performance.

This page serves as the main management overview of the report.

### 4.2 Customer Insights

The Customer Insights page focuses on understanding the characteristics and behavior of the customer base.

The analysis provides insights into customer demographics, tenure, contract types, customer segments, and churn patterns.

This page helps identify customer groups that may require targeted retention strategies.

### 4.3 Service Analysis

The Service Analysis page examines the relationship between the services customers use and their likelihood of churning.

It provides insights into service adoption and identifies services or service combinations associated with higher or lower churn levels.

This analysis can help management evaluate service-related customer experience and retention opportunities.

### 4.4 Revenue Analysis

The Revenue Analysis page focuses on the financial impact of customer churn.

It analyzes customer charges, revenue-related metrics, and the potential financial implications of customer attrition.

This page helps connect customer churn with business revenue and highlights areas where revenue may be at risk.

### 4.5 View True Customer Details

The View True Customer Details page provides a more detailed customer-level view of the dataset.

This page supports deeper investigation of individual customer records and can be used to identify specific customers or customer characteristics requiring further attention.

The analysis identified important patterns relating to customer churn, customer characteristics, services, and revenue.

- Customer segments with higher churn exposure.

## 9. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management can use the findings to:

- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-risk customer segments.
- Monitor customers showing characteristics associated with higher churn risk.
- Review service offerings associated with increased customer attrition.
- Introduce targeted incentives and retention initiatives where appropriate.
- Monitor revenue at risk and prioritize high-value customers for retention.
- Use customer data and churn indicators to support proactive customer engagement.

The detailed recommendations and supporting analysis are provided in the Executive Insights Report.
